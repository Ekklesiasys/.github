## What We Build

Ekklesiasys is an all-in-one church management platform built for the realities of ministry life in Ghana and across Africa. We give church leaders and administrators a single, unified system so they can spend less time on operations and more time on ministry.

Our platform covers every layer of how a modern church runs:

- **Membership and Pastoral Care** — member profiles, family groupings, attendance tracking, growth pathways, and pastoral notes
- **Online Giving** — tithes, offerings, pledges, and special projects with Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe, PayPal, and Monnify
- **Communication** — bulk SMS, email, and WhatsApp outreach with member segmentation and scheduling
- **Events and Scheduling** — calendar management, digital check-in, event registration, and volunteer coordination
- **Analytics and Reporting** — real-time KPI dashboards, growth and retention metrics, and exportable reports
- **Apps and Portals** — dedicated mobile and web experiences for members, finance teams, children's ministry, and volunteers
- **Website Builder** — custom church websites delivered and integrated directly with your EkklesiaSys account
